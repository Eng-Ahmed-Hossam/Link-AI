# End-of-pilot interview and pricing · مقابلة نهاية التجربة والسعر

> Draft — review before use. With the **owner** (who decides to pay), 30–40 minutes, after `pnpm pilot:metrics` and before `pnpm pilot:wipe`. Reception and teachers: questions 1–6 only, separately.

## Rules for us (so we don't bias the answer)

- **Ask about what happened, not what they think of us.** Past behaviour beats opinions.
- **Don't pitch, don't explain features, don't defend.** If they criticise, ask «زي إيه؟» (*like what?*) and write it down.
- **Silence is fine.** Wait after each question; don't fill it with options.
- **Don't name the price before section C.** Don't mention "discount", "early bird" or "cheap".
- Note **their exact words**, then the metric that goes with it.
- One of us asks, the other writes.

## A. What happened · اللي حصل

1. «احكيلي عن آخر مرة طالب غاب أكتر من حصة **قبل** التجربة. عرفتوا إمتى؟ عملتوا إيه؟» — *Tell me about the last time, before the pilot, that a student missed more than one session. When did you find out? What did you do?*
2. «وفي التجربة: احكيلي عن متابعة واحدة من أولها لآخرها.» — *During the pilot: walk me through one follow-up from start to end.*
3. «إيه اللي اتغيّر في شغلكم الأسبوع ده، لو في حاجة اتغيّرت؟» — *What changed in your work this week, if anything?*

## B. Value and what they would stop · القيمة وما سيتوقفون عنه

4. «لو شلنا Link بكرة، إيه أول حاجة هتفتقدوها؟» — *If we took Link away tomorrow, what would you miss first?*
5. «وإيه اللي مش هتفتقدوه خالص؟» — *And what wouldn't you miss at all?*
6. «لو استمر، إيه اللي هتبطّلوا تعملوه بالطريقة القديمة؟ (ورق، دفتر، واتساب شخصي…)» — *If it continued, what would you stop doing the old way (paper, notebook, personal WhatsApp…)?*
7. «مين في المركز هيستخدمه كل يوم؟ ومين لأ؟» — *Who at the centre would use it every day? Who wouldn't?*
8. Show the metrics (counts, not percentages first): «ده اللي اتسجّل الأسبوع ده. ده شبه اللي حصل فعلًا؟» — *This is what was recorded this week. Does it match what really happened?*

## C. Price · السعر

Say it once, plainly, then stop talking:

> «السعر اللي بنفكر فيه **٢٬٠٠٠ جنيه في الشهر للمركز** — يشمل المتابعة دي لكل المجموعات والمعلّمين.»
> *"The price we have in mind is **EGP 2,000 per month for the centre**, covering this follow-up for all groups and teachers."* (OD-53)

9. «إيه رأيك؟» — then **wait.** Write the first reaction word for word.
10. «لو بدأنا الشهر الجاي بالسعر ده، مين في المركز يقرر؟ وإيه اللي محتاجين تشوفوه قبلها؟» — *If we started next month at this price, who at the centre decides? What would you need to see first?*
11. If the reaction is "too expensive": «إيه اللي يخليه يستاهل السعر ده؟» — *What would make it worth this price?* (Don't lower the price in the room.)
12. If the reaction is positive, make **one concrete ask** — choose before the meeting, and ask only one:
    - **Paid first month:** «تحبوا نبدأ الشهر الجاي؟ الدفع أول الشهر.» — *Shall we start next month? Payment at the start of the month.*
    - **Deposit:** «نحجز مكانكم بعربون [المبلغ]، ويتخصم من أول شهر.» — *We hold your place with a deposit of [amount], deducted from the first month.*
    - **Letter of intent:** «تمضوا خطاب نية إنكم هتشتركوا بالسعر ده لما نطلق؟» — *Would you sign a letter of intent to subscribe at this price at launch?*

## D. Close · الختام

13. «في حد تاني — مركز أو مدرّس — تفتكر لازم نكلّمه؟» — *Anyone else — a centre or a teacher — we should talk to?*
14. Confirm deletion: «هنمسح كل بيانات التجربة خلال يومين ونبعتلكم إيصال الحذف.» — *We'll delete all pilot data within two days and send you the deletion receipt.*
