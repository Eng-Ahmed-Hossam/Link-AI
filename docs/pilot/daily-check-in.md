# Daily check-in · المتابعة اليومية

> Draft — review before use. For the founder, once a day, ~10 minutes, at the end of the day. Ask in Arabic; note answers in their words. **Do not help or correct during the day unless something is broken** — what staff do on their own is the result.

## Before you ask (2 minutes, from the laptop)

- [ ] `pnpm pilot:metrics` → today's row: sessions, records confirmed, flags, contacts, outcomes.
- [ ] A01 Today: anything overdue? Any follow-up with no contact yet?
- [ ] The server window: any error, restart, or "port in use" since yesterday?

## Ask Reception · الاستقبال

1. «إيه أول حاجة عملتيها النهارده على Link؟» — *What was the first thing you did on Link today?*
2. «في متابعة مقدرتيش تخلّصيها؟ ليه؟» — *Was there a follow-up you couldn't finish? Why?*
3. «الرسالة اللي اقترحها البرنامج — غيّرتي فيها حاجة قبل ما تبعتيها؟ إيه؟» — *Did you change the suggested message before sending? What?*
4. «ردّ حد من أولياء الأمور؟ سجّلتي الرد؟» — *Did any guardian reply? Did you log it?*
5. «كان في حاجة محتاجة وقت أكتر من المفروض؟» — *Did anything take longer than it should?*

## Ask each teacher · المعلّم

1. «سجّلت الحضور بعد كل حصة النهارده؟ لو لأ، ليه؟» — *Did you record every session today? If not, why?*
2. «كام دقيقة بياخد تسجيل الحصة؟» — *How many minutes does a record take?*
3. «في حاجة في الشاشة مش واضحة أو مزعجة؟» — *Anything unclear or annoying on screen?*
4. (If asked to correct) «طلب التصحيح وصلك؟ كان صح؟» — *Did the correction request reach you? Was it right?*

## Ask the owner (every other day) · المالك

1. «بصّيت على صفحة اليوم؟ حسّيت إنك عارف مين محتاج متابعة؟» — *Did you look at Today? Did you feel you knew who needed follow-up?*
2. «في حاجة كنت عايز تشوفها ومش لاقيها؟» — *Anything you wanted to see and couldn't find?*

## Observe (don't ask)

- Who opens the laptop first, and when? Do they open it without being reminded?
- Does Reception send from the centre's WhatsApp right after approving, or later?
- Workarounds: paper lists, notes in their own WhatsApp, skipping a step.
- Moments of confusion: where do they stop or ask someone?
