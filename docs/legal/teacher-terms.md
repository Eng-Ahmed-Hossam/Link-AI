# Terms for teachers — DRAFT for lawyer review

> Draft · part of `terms` · from MKT-TCH, BR-VER-03/04, BR-REF-01, BR-OUT and OD-02 (docs/01, docs/13).

1. **Joining is free.** Before your profile is public, before you accept students and before any payout, Link checks your national ID (by video call until automatic ID checks arrive). A degree and two references are optional badges Link verifies.
2. **Your groups and fees.** You set your fee per month or per session. Parents pay your fee through Link and pay no extra fee. A change of fee applies to new places only.
3. **Booking commission.** Link keeps **[5]%** of each fee you receive, taken from the payment; you see it on its own line in Earnings. If a parent is refunded before the first session, the commission is returned too.
4. **When you are paid.** Your money is held until the place is confirmed and the first session of the paid period has started. Link pays your available balance weekly (Thursday) to your verified bank account or wallet, holding back the hall rent built up so far in the month.
5. **Hall rent.** You rent hall time from centres through Link. The month's rent is taken from your balance on the 1st of the next month; if your balance is short, you pay the rest through Link within 5 days.
6. **Cancelling.** If you cancel a group or end it early, the parents are refunded for sessions not delivered, from your balance.
7. **Chargebacks.** A chargeback is taken from your unpaid balance; if the money was already paid out, Link recovers it from your future balances.
8. **Follow-up (when the centre uses it).** Your voice notes are turned into draft records that you check and confirm; nothing is saved to a student without your confirmation, and no message reaches a parent without staff approval. Voice recordings are deleted after 30 days.
9. **Reviews.** You can reply to or report a review; you cannot hide or delete it.
