# Terms for centres — DRAFT for lawyer review

> Draft · part of `terms` · from MKT-CEN, BR-VER, BR-OUT and OD-01/OD-12 (docs/01, docs/13).

1. **Joining is free.** You ask to join ("Add my centre"); Link calls you within 2 working days, checks the address and map pin, visits or holds a video call, checks the owner's ID, and asks you to approve your public page. You go live only after every check.
2. **Halls and rent.** You list halls, their capacity and the rent rule (a fixed amount per session, an amount per student per session, or a percentage of the teacher's fees). You approve or decline each teacher's request; nothing is booked without you, unless you switch on auto-approve with your own rules.
3. **Marketing fee.** Link keeps **[5]%** of the hall rent (allowed range 5–10%, agreed per centre), taken before payout. Joining, listing and requests cost nothing.
4. **How rent is paid.** Rent for a calendar month is invoiced on the 1st of the next month and taken from the teacher's balance at once. Link pays your available balance weekly (Thursday) to a verified bank account or wallet. If a teacher's balance does not cover the rent, the teacher pays the rest through Link within 5 days.
5. **Sessions that did not take place.** Until the month's invoice is issued, you or the teacher can mark a session "did not take place" with a reason; it is never charged.
6. **Reviews.** Parents with confirmed places may review your centre. You can reply or report; you cannot hide or delete a review.
7. **Your staff.** You invite staff by phone number and choose what each may do. You are responsible for your staff's use of Link.
8. **Students' data.** You see the students booked in your halls only as your role allows. With Link Follow-up, you decide what is recorded about your students; Link processes it for you (see [privacy](privacy.md) §3).
9. **Verification can be revoked** if the checks no longer hold (for example the address changed and was not re-checked, or fraud). Your page is hidden and payouts pause until it is resolved; Link tells you why.
