# Terms of use — DRAFT for lawyer review

> Draft · label `terms` · not approved (OD-60) · not legal advice. Arabic is the binding language once approved; this English draft is the working copy.

## 1. Who we are

Link ("we") runs a marketplace for tutoring in Egypt: parents find teachers and groups and reserve seats; teachers rent hall time in tutoring centres; centres list halls. Company name, commercial register number and address: **[to fill when the company exists]**. Contact: **[contact email]**.

## 2. Accounts

- You sign in with a mobile number and a one-time code. Keep your phone secure; actions from your account are yours.
- One account per person. You may hold several roles (for example parent and teacher).
- Give true information. We may suspend an account used for fraud, abuse or to break these terms, and tell you why.

## 3. What Link does and does not do

- Link connects parents, teachers and centres and handles the payments between them. **The teacher teaches; the centre provides the hall.** Link does not employ teachers and does not run the classes.
- Centres appear on the map only after Link verifies them (a call, the address and pin, a visit or video call, the owner's ID, and the owner's approval of the profile). Teachers' profiles are public only after an ID check.
- Badges (for example "Sends progress updates") come from Link's own data; nobody can buy or set them.

## 4. Payments go through Link

- Every payment between parents, teachers and centres goes through Link: card, Fawry or a mobile wallet. There is no "pay in cash at the desk" route for a Link booking.
- Card details are entered on the payment provider's page. Link never sees or stores your card number.
- A monthly plan renews on the same day each month by card and can be cancelled at any time; cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps the month already paid.
- A booking is confirmed only when the provider confirms the payment to Link (not when the browser returns).

## 5. Fees

- **Parents pay no booking fee.** The price shown is the teacher's price.
- **Teachers** pay Link a booking commission of **[5]%** of the fee, taken from the payment before payout.
- **Centres** pay Link a marketing fee of **[5–10]%** of the hall rent, taken from the rent before payout.
- Rates in force are the ones shown when the payment is made; a change applies to new payments only. Link rounds its fee down to the piaster.
- Link pays the payment provider's processing fees.
- Paid extras (Link Follow-up) are a separate subscription with its own price, shown before you subscribe.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds

See the [refund policy](refund-policy.md). In short: a full refund if you cancel before the first session; after that, Link reviews the request as a dispute; if the teacher cancels, you are refunded for the sessions not delivered.

## 7. Payouts to teachers and centres

Link holds a teacher's money until the seat is confirmed and the first session of the paid period has started, then pays available balances weekly (Thursday) to a verified bank account or wallet. Details: [teacher terms](teacher-terms.md), [centre terms](centre-terms.md).

## 8. Reviews

- Only a parent with a confirmed place, after the first session, can review — once per term.
- Criticism of teaching is welcome. Insults, threats, personal details and phone numbers are not; such a review is held for Link to check within 48 hours.
- The centre or teacher may reply or report a review; **nobody but Link can hide one, and nobody can delete one.**

## 9. Link Follow-up (paid extra)

When a centre switches it on, teachers record short notes after class, Link turns them into records the teacher confirms, and staff approve every message before it reaches a parent. Nothing is sent to a parent automatically. Parents receive WhatsApp or SMS updates only if they opted in, and can stop at any time by replying STOP.

## 10. Your conduct

Do not: use Link to take payments outside it; post false information or someone else's details; harass anyone; scrape or attack the service; upload anything you have no right to.
