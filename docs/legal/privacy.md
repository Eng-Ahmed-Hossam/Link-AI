## 3. Children's data and Follow-up

- Children's details come only from their guardian, with the guardian's explicit consent when the child is added.
- **Follow-up records are the centre's.** When a centre uses Link Follow-up, Link processes those records on the centre's behalf. Teachers confirm every record; staff approve every message before it reaches a parent; nothing is sent automatically.
- Voice notes are encrypted and **deleted after 30 days**. Transcripts are deleted after 90 days. Before any automatic processing, names are replaced with codes and phone numbers are removed.
- Parents see approved messages only, never transcripts.

## 4. Who sees what

- **The centre and the teacher** see the bookings in their groups; the teacher sees a parent's phone only if the parent agreed at checkout.
- **Each centre sees only its own data.** This is enforced in the database itself.
- **Service providers**, only what each needs: the payment provider (payments and refunds), the SMS provider (sign-in codes), WhatsApp (approved updates, only if you opted in), the hosting provider. **[List the providers chosen; see docs/plan/provider-choice.md.]**
- **Link staff** with a specific permission (verification, review moderation, refunds); every view and action is logged.
- We do not sell personal data and do not show advertising.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask for **a copy** of your data, **a correction**, or **deletion** of your account, and you can withdraw any consent. In the parent app: **Account → My data**; or write to **[privacy email]**. Link answers within **30 days**. Withdrawing WhatsApp updates is immediate: reply **STOP**.

Deletion removes your name, phone and children's details. Payment and audit records the law requires us to keep stay, without your name.

You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Centre **[contact]**.

## 8. Security

Phones and payout accounts are encrypted in the database; voice notes are encrypted at rest; each centre's data is isolated; card data never reaches Link. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities within the legal time limit **[OD-27]**.

When this notice changes we tell you in the app and ask you to read it again.
