# Cancellations and refunds — DRAFT for lawyer review

> Draft · part of `terms` · from business rules BR-REF-01…08 and BR-PMT-05/06 (docs/01).

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| You cancel **before the first session** of the paid period | **Full refund**, including Link's commission. Your place is released at once. Link approves the refund (usually the same working day) and the money returns to your card, Fawry or wallet. |
| You cancel **after the first session** | No automatic refund. Ask Link from the booking page; Link reviews it as a dispute with the centre and the teacher and tells you the decision with the reason. |
| You cancel a **monthly plan** | No further renewals. The month you already paid stays yours. |
| The **teacher cancels** the group or ends it early | Refund for every session not delivered, worked out per session. |
| The **teacher declines** your child's place | Full refund, automatic. |
| Your payment **arrives after the place was taken** (for example a late Fawry payment) | Full refund, automatic. |
| A renewal **fails** | We retry after 1 day and after 3 days and tell you each time. If it is still unpaid when the new month's first session starts, the place is kept for 7 days, then released. |

- Refunds go back to the method you paid with when the provider allows it; otherwise to a wallet or bank account you give us.
- The time the money takes to appear depends on your bank or wallet (often 5–14 days for cards).
- Every refund is recorded and the parent, the teacher and the centre are told.
