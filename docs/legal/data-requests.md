# Answering data requests (PDPL) — ops procedure, DRAFT

> For Link ops with `ops.verify`. The console counts **30 days** from the request (limit to confirm with the lawyer). Every step is in the audit log.

People ask from **Account → My data** (or by email: create the request for them only after checking it is really them — a call from the account's phone number).

| Request | What you do | What you write in "What was done" |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy** (`access`) | Press **Download the data (JSON)** on the request: the account, children, bookings, payments, refunds, reviews and consents (the download is audited). Send it to the account's phone owner by a secure link. | "Exported account, N bookings, N payments; sent on <date> by <how>" |
| **Correction** | Correct the field (name, child's details) with the person on the phone. Money records are never edited; a wrong amount is corrected with a balanced adjustment (ops.finance). | "Child's name corrected from … to …" (no phone numbers) |
| **Deletion** | Check there is no money owed either way and no open dispute. Anonymise: name → "Deleted user", phone removed, children's names removed, reviews' text removed (stars stay in the rating), WhatsApp consents withdrawn. Keep payments, ledger, invoices and the audit log (the law requires them), without the name. | "Account anonymised; N payments kept without name" |
| **Refuse** | Only with a legal reason (for example, an open dispute, or we cannot confirm identity). | The reason, in words the person will understand |

Never paste a phone number, national ID or child's full name into the outcome or a note.
